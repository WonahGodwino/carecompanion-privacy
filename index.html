CareCompanion Privacy Policy

Excellence Community Education Welfare Scheme (ECEWS)

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Developer/Organisation: Excellence Community Education Welfare Scheme (ECEWS)

Application: CareCompanion

CareCompanion (the “App”) is a mobile tool used by authorised Frontline healthcare workers to support the care and follow-up of clients receiving HIV/AIDS and related health services in the supported facilities. This policy is based on the App’s actual data-processing functionality.

1. Data collection

CareCompanion does not collect or transmit user data off the mobile device, based on its current implementation.

The App retrieves patient and clinical records, including existing biometric eligibility information, from the designated facility CareCompanion server for authorised healthcare workers to view and use in delivering healthcare services.

The App does not:

  • Capture fingerprints or generate fingerprint templates.
  • Upload patient health records or personal information from the mobile device.
  • Transmit biometric eligibility information from the mobile device.
  • Transmit GPS coordinates for geofence verification.
  • Transmit staff usernames or user IDs during login.
  • Upload outreach call activity or call logs.
  • Use Firebase, Crashlytics, Google Analytics or other external SDKs to collect or transmit user data.

2. Data sharing

CareCompanion does not share user data with third-party service providers, advertising networks or external analytics services.

Patient information retrieved by the App is obtained from the designated facility CareCompanion server through the authorised facility network.

3. Data security

  • Patient records are accessed through the authorised facility network and are not intended to be permanently stored on the mobile device.
  • Geofence verification is performed locally on the device.
  • Outreach call activity and other specified operational information remain stored locally.

4. Biometric information

CareCompanion does not capture fingerprints, generate fingerprint templates or perform biometric enrolment.

The App only retrieves and displays existing biometric eligibility information from the facility system to assist authorised healthcare workers in determining whether biometric enrolment, recapturing or further verification is required.

5. Advertising and analytics

CareCompanion does not use patient health information for advertising and does not integrate Firebase, Crashlytics, Google Analytics or other external analytics SDKs for collecting user data.
