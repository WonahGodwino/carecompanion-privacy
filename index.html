CareCompanion (the “App”) is a mobile tool used by authorised Frontline healthcare workers to support the care and follow-up of clients receiving HIV/AIDS and related health services in the supported facilities. This policy explains what data the App processes, why, and how we protect it.

1. Data we collect

The App is used by trained health staff (case managers, clinicians) at health facilities. It may process the following categories of data:

CategoryExamples
Health dataHIV status, ART regimen, viral load results, appointment history, PMTCT/EID/TB/TPT service records
Personal informationClient names, phone numbers, client identification codes
Biometric dataFingerprint templates captured is used solely for biometric eligibility tracking to aid the Frontline healthcare workers
Location dataApproximate/precise device location, used only to confirm the device is within the authorised facility perimeter (geofence)
Device/usage dataDevice model, app version, crash diagnostics, support tickets
Call activityRecords of outreach calls made to clients through the App

2. How we use data

  • To provide and coordinate HIV/ART care and follow-up services for clients.
  • To identify clients accurately and safely (biometric matching).
  • To generate service worklists (refills, viral load, appointments, follow-ups) for health workers.
  • To provide in-app notifications (on-device) for appointments and follow-up tasks.
  • To monitor service quality and produce aggregate reports.
  • To provide technical support and improve the App.
Purpose limitation. The App is solely for patient management and tracking by case managers, in line with ECEWS service guidelines, and must not be used for any other purpose without written permission from ECEWS.

We do not use health data for advertising, and we do not sell personal data.

3. Legal basis

We process personal and health data on the basis of:

  • The provision of health care and public-health functions;
  • The legitimate interests of the health programme (with appropriate safeguards); and/or
  • Consent, where applicable.

4. Data sharing

Data does not leave the facility system. The App is not hosted by, and does not share data with, any central server or third-party service provider outside the facility. Data is shared only as necessary for care delivery:

  • With the health facility and its authorised staff;
  • Where required by law or a competent authority.

5. Data storage & security

  • Patient and clinical records are not retained on the device. They are loaded into memory from the facility’s CareCompanion server while the device is connected to the facility network, and are discarded when that connection ends.
  • Data is synchronised with the facility’s CareCompanion server over the facility’s local network (LAN). This network may not have internet connectivity. All communication is encrypted.
  • Non-clinical operational data (such as app settings, login activity and call logs) may be stored locally on the device.
  • Credentials and secrets are stored using Android encrypted storage.
  • The App blocks screenshots and screen recording (FLAG_SECURE) to reduce exposure of on-screen patient information.
  • Access is restricted to authenticated, authorised users with role-based permissions.

6. Data retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes above and in line with applicable health-record retention requirements. Records are retained in accordance with ECEWS service guidelines and applicable national health-records policy.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, clients and users may have rights to:

  • Access, correct, or request deletion of their data;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Request data portability;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact the facility or the programme’s data protection contact below.

8. Children

The App is intended for use by trained healthcare staff and is not directed at children. Minors’ data is processed only in the course of clinical care provided by authorised staff.
